Frequently Asked Questions

How do I edit my birth details?

Go to the Account tab → Birth Details section → tap Edit next to the field you want to update. Note: updating your birth details will clear your cached chart and regenerate it on your next visit.

My daily forecast isn't loading. What should I do?

Make sure you have an active internet connection. If the issue persists, try signing out and signing back in. If it still doesn't work, contact us at desiantobs@gmail.com.

How do I cancel my Lintara Pro subscription?

Lintara Pro is managed through Apple's App Store. To cancel:

  • Open Settings on your iPhone
  • Tap your name → Subscriptions
  • Select Lintara → Cancel Subscription

How do I delete my account?

Go to the Account tab → scroll to the bottom → tap Delete account data. This will permanently remove your account and all associated data.

I found a bug. How do I report it?

Please email us at desiantobs@gmail.com with a description of the bug and steps to reproduce it. We appreciate your help in improving Lintara!
