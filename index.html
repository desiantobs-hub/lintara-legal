Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 16, 2026

Lintara ("we", "our", or "us") is a personal astrology app developed by Desianto Budi Setiaji. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Lintara, we collect the following information:

  • Name and Email Address — collected via Google Sign-In when you create an account.
  • Date of Birth — used to calculate your natal chart and generate personalized astrological readings.
  • Birth Time — used to improve the accuracy of your natal chart (optional).
  • Birth City / Location — used to determine your rising sign and house positions.
  • Journal Entries — text you write in the in-app journal. Used to generate AI reflections personalized to your planetary transits.
  • User Preferences — such as relationship status, work situation, and current focus. Used to personalize your daily forecast.
  • User ID — a unique identifier assigned to your account, used to manage your data securely.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate your natal chart, daily forecast, and weekly reading.
  • To provide AI-powered journal reflections based on your planetary transits.
  • To calculate compatibility readings when you add other people.
  • To personalize your experience based on your preferences.
  • To maintain and improve the app.

We do not use your data for advertising or sell it to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a cloud database provider. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

4. Third-Party Services

Lintara uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Sign-In — for authentication. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase — for data storage. Subject to Supabase's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat — for managing in-app subscriptions. Subject to RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
  • AI Language Models — used to generate personalized astrological readings and journal reflections. Content sent to AI providers is not used to train their models.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except as required to provide the services described above or as required by law.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data.
  • Withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account.

To exercise these rights, tap Delete account data in the Account section of the app, or contact us at desiantobs@gmail.com.

7. Children's Privacy

Lintara is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
